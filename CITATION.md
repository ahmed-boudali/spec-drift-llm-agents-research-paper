## Authorship note

Author order and co-authorship are **provisional**, pending the supervisor's

## What may and may not be cited

- **The drift audit (E1)** is a completed measurement. Its figures are
  reproducible from source and may be cited as results.
- **The projections in the paper's Section 7.2** are labelled
  `[ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT MEASURED]`. They are not data and must not be cited as
  findings.
